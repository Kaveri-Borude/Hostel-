Hostel Information




Welcome to our hostel in the PVP College campus environment. It is a safe and friendly place for students to stay, study, and grow. We provide clean rooms, study spaces, Wi‑Fi, and healthy food in the dining hall.

The hostel has 24/7 security, wardens, and a peaceful atmosphere so students can focus on their studies. Being inside the college campus makes it easy to reach classes, library, and other facilities. Our hostel is not just a place to live — it is a supportive home where students learn discipline, respect, and build good friendships.

Study Room

Quiet study areas with Wi‑Fi and proper lighting to help students focus on their learning.Modern design with spacious rooms and balconies, providing comfort and safety for students.

Mess Facility

Healthy and hygienic food served daily in a clean dining hall with seating for all students.

Play Ground

Open ground for sports and activities, encouraging fitness and recreation alongside academics.

Bed Room

Comfortable and well‑furnished bedrooms with proper ventilation, storage space, and a peaceful environment for rest and relaxation.”



Hostel Facilities

  • Spacious Rooms

    Well-ventilated rooms with comfortable beds, study tables, and storage facilities.

  • Dining Hall

    Nutritious meals served at fixed timings in a clean and hygienic dining area.

  • Wi-Fi Connectivity

    High-speed internet access available throughout the hostel premises.

  • Study Area

    Dedicated quiet zones and common study halls for group discussions and learning.

Hostel Rules

  1. General Conduct

    Residents must maintain discipline and respect toward staff and fellow students.

    Loud music, parties, and disruptive behavior are strictly prohibited.

  2. Timings

    Entry and exit are allowed only between 6:00 AM to 6:00 PM.

    Late entries require prior written permission from the warden.

  3. Cleanliness

    Rooms and common areas must be kept clean and tidy.

    Littering in corridors, bathrooms, or outdoor spaces is not allowed.

  4. Visitors

    Visitors are allowed only during designated hours and must register at the front desk.

    Overnight stays by guests are not permitted.

  5. Safety & Security

    Residents must carry their hostel ID at all times.

    Tampering with fire safety equipment or security systems is a serious offense.

  6. Food & Cooking

    Cooking inside rooms is strictly prohibited.

  7. Prohibited Items

    Electrical appliances like heaters or induction cooktops are not allowed in rooms.

  8. Damages

    Any damage to hostel property must be reported and compensated by the resident.

  9. Attendance

    Daily attendance may be taken for safety and accountability.

  10. Disciplinary Action

    Violation of rules may lead to warnings, fines, or cancellation of hostel accommodation.

  11. Ragging

    Ragging in any Form is strictly Prohibited.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. What are the hostel timings?

Entry and exit are allowed only between 6:00 AM to 6:00 PM. Late entries require prior permission from the warden.

2. Can visitors stay overnight?

No, overnight stays by guests are not permitted. Visitors are allowed only during designated hours and must register at the front desk.

3. Is Wi-Fi available in the hostel?

Yes, high-speed Wi-Fi connectivity is available throughout the hostel premises.

4. What facilities are provided in the rooms?

Rooms are equipped with beds, study tables, chairs, and storage facilities. Each room is well-ventilated and maintained regularly.

5. How do I report maintenance issues?

Residents can report issues directly to the hostel office or through the online student portal. Maintenance staff will address them promptly.

Privacy Policy

1. Information We Collect

We collect basic personal details such as name, contact information, and admission records to provide hostel services.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used for admissions, room allocation, communication, and safety purposes. We do not share your data with unauthorized parties.

3. Data Security

We implement strict security measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, misuse, or disclosure.

4. Sharing of Information

We may share information with government authorities or emergency services when legally required or for safety reasons.

5. Student Rights

Students have the right to request deletion of their personal data by contacting the hostel administration.

6. Policy Updates

This privacy policy may be updated periodically. Students will be notified of significant changes through the hostel notice board or portal.
